# C54.6 — Logical Coordinate Capture Hotfix

Signature: Tehkné Solutions

## Human review result

The C54.5 machine gate passed, but the regenerated 1920×1080 capture remained visually invalid. The two cyan gameplay envelopes and fighters were still displaced toward the bottom and right edges, with clipping visible in the proof image.

## Root cause

The active project viewport is 1280×720 and the captured output is stretched to 1920×1080. Applying a transform directly to the root `Node2D` did not affect the root scene content as expected during this capture path. Authored coordinates were therefore stretched by 1.5 without first being mapped into logical viewport coordinates.

## Correction

The bench now:

- keeps the canonical authored canvas at 1920×1080;
- computes the logical viewport scale and offset;
- maps every point, rectangle, font size, sprite scale and baseline into logical coordinates explicitly;
- retires root-level layout transform assignments;
- validates the logical gameplay height separately from the authored 132 px target;
- verifies that both logical cyan envelopes are enclosed by the viewport;
- scans the final PNG for cyan envelope bounds so a dimension-only capture cannot pass again.

## Promotion policy

- BASE-00 art and SHA-256 remain unchanged;
- C54 human review remains blocked until a new complete capture is generated;
- BASE-01 must not begin before the regenerated proof shows both complete fighters, both cyan envelopes, the shared green baseline and the lower labels inside frame;
- project progress remains unchanged.
